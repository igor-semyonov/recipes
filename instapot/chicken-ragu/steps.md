# Chicken Ragù — Steps

Grate the onions and carrots on a box grater before you start. That's what makes the vegetables vanish — no blending needed later.

## 1. Skillet

Separate pan, not Instant Pot Sauté mode.

1. 1/4 cup olive oil over medium-high.
2. 1 tsp fennel seeds — 30–45 sec, until fragrant and slightly darkened. **Don't exceed a minute.**
3. Grated onion, grated carrot, chopped mushrooms — 8–10 min. Cook until the mushroom water has released *and* cooked off and things start to brown. Don't rush this; that liquid is reduction you'd otherwise do later.
4. Garlic — 30 sec.
5. Dried basil, oregano, thyme, salt, pepper, and 1/4 cup tomato paste — 1–2 min, until the paste darkens to brick red.
6. 1/2 cup red wine — scrape the fond loose and reduce until nearly dry, ~2 min.

## 2. Load the pot — order matters

1. 1/2 cup water or broth
2. 1/2 cup dal, rinsed — stir to suspend
3. Skillet contents, scraped in
4. Spinach, pressed under
5. Chicken thighs, whole, pushed down into the mixture
6. Bay leaves + Parmesan rinds
7. Tomatoes, crushed by hand, poured over the top — **do not stir**

## 3. Cook

High pressure **15 min**. Natural release **15 min**, then vent.

## 4. Reduce — the step that makes it a sauce

1. Remove bay leaves and Parmesan rinds.
2. **Pull the chicken out to a bowl and shred it.** Leaving it out lets you reduce hard without overcooking the meat.
3. Mash the sauce with a potato masher until the vegetables disappear into it.
4. Sauté mode, lid off, **15–25 min**, stirring often. Use a splatter guard. Done when it mounds on a spoon instead of running off.
5. Return the shredded chicken, warm through.

## 5. Finish off heat

1. 3 Tbsp butter, stirred in until melted.
2. Torn fresh basil.
3. Taste. Balsamic or a pinch of sugar **only** if sharp.
4. Salt last.

## 6. Serve

Over pasta or rice. Parmesan, basil, and the chili options on the table.

## 7. Profit

Serves 10–12. Freezes well. Margin undisclosed.
