# Chicken Ragù — Ingredients

8 qt Instant Pot. Serves 10–12 over pasta or rice.

## Shopping list

**Produce**
- 2 large yellow onions
- 3 carrots
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms
- 1 head garlic (need 8 cloves)
- 5 oz fresh spinach
- Fresh basil, 1 large bunch

**Meat**
- 2.5–3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs

**Canned / jarred**
- 2 (28 oz) cans whole peeled tomatoes — **not diced**
- 1 small can/tube tomato paste (need 1/4 cup)

**Dairy**
- Butter (need 3 Tbsp)
- Parmesan — a wedge, plus rinds if you have them saved
- Greek yogurt or ricotta, optional, for serving

**Pantry / dry**
- Moong dal or red lentils (need 1/2 cup)
- Olive oil (need 1/4 cup)
- Dry red wine (need 1/2 cup)
- Chicken broth (need 1/2 cup)
- Fennel seeds, whole
- Dried basil
- Dried oregano
- Dried thyme
- Bay leaves
- Balsamic vinegar
- Salt, black pepper

**For the table (adults)**
- Red pepper flakes, chili oil, or Calabrian chili paste

**Serving**
- Pasta or rice

---

## Amounts by stage

### Skillet

- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 tsp fennel seeds, whole
- 2 large onions, grated
- 3 carrots, grated
- 8 oz cremini mushrooms, finely chopped
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup tomato paste
- 1/2 cup dry red wine

### Dried herbs and seasoning

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Dried basil | 1 Tbsp |
| Dried oregano | 2 tsp |
| Dried thyme | 1 tsp |
| Bay leaves | 2 |
| Salt | 2 tsp to start |
| Black pepper | to taste |

### Into the pot

- 1/2 cup water or chicken broth — bottom layer, burn insurance
- 1/2 cup moong dal or red lentils, rinsed
- 5 oz fresh spinach
- 2.5–3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, whole
- 1–2 Parmesan rinds, optional
- 2 (28 oz) cans whole peeled tomatoes, crushed by hand — on top, unstirred

### Off heat, at the end

- 3 Tbsp butter or good olive oil
- Large handful fresh basil, torn
- 1 Tbsp balsamic, or a pinch of sugar — **only if it tastes sharp**
- Salt to taste

### Table

- Grated Parmesan
- Fresh basil
- **Red pepper flakes / chili oil / Calabrian chili paste — adults only**

The sauce itself contains no capsaicin. All heat lives on the table.

Carrots and dal both soften tomato acidity, so you will likely need less sugar than a typical recipe calls for. Taste before reaching for it.
