# Chicken Tortilla Soup — Steps

Measure 6.5 cups broth up front. Set 1 cup aside for the skillet; the other 5.5 go in the pot.

## 1. Skillet

Sauté in a separate pan, not Instant Pot Sauté mode — better browning, more even heat.

1. 2 Tbsp olive oil over medium-high.
2. 1 tsp cumin seeds + 1 tsp coriander seeds — 30–45 sec, until they darken a shade and smell nutty. **Do not exceed a minute; burnt cumin is bitter and unfixable.**
3. Onion, bell pepper, carrots — 5 min.
4. Garlic — 30 sec.
5. Ground spices, salt, and 3 Tbsp tomato paste — 1 min, until fragrant and the paste darkens.
6. Pour in the reserved 1 cup broth and scrape every bit of fond loose.

## 2. Load the pot — order matters

1. 5.5 cups broth
2. 1/2 cup moong dal, rinsed — stir to suspend, don't leave it piled on the bottom
3. Skillet contents, scraped in
4. Spinach, pressed under the liquid
5. Chicken thighs, whole and submerged
6. 2 bay leaves
7. Diced tomatoes + green chiles poured over the top — **do not stir**

## 3. Cook

High pressure **12 min**. Natural release **10–15 min**, then vent.

## 4. Finish

1. Remove bay leaves. Pull the chicken, shred, return it.
2. Ladle out ~1.5 cups of mostly-liquid. Tear in 3 corn tortillas, soak 2–3 min, blend smooth **while hot**, stir back in.
3. Stir in frozen corn and rinsed black beans. Sauté mode 3–5 min to heat through and let the starch set up.
4. Off heat: juice of 2 limes, **then** salt to taste. Lime first.
5. Thin with hot broth if it's tighter than you want.

## 5. Garnish

Set out the garnish bar and let everyone build their own bowl. Jalapeños stay on the adult end of the table.

## 6. Profit

Serves 8–10. Margin undisclosed.
