# Chicken Tortilla Soup — Ingredients

8 qt Instant Pot. Serves 8–10.

## Base

- 2.5–3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs (whole, shredded after)
- 6.5 cups low-sodium chicken broth — **reserve 1 cup for deglazing the skillet**
- 1/2 cup moong dal, rinsed
- 2 Tbsp olive oil
- 1 large yellow onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 carrots, diced small
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 Tbsp tomato paste
- 1 (28 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 1 (4 oz) can mild diced green chiles

## Spices

Whole seeds bloom in the oil first; ground spices go in with the tomato paste.

| Spice | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Cumin seeds, whole | 1 tsp |
| Coriander seeds, whole | 1 tsp |
| Cumin, ground | 2 tsp |
| Ancho chili powder | 1 Tbsp |
| Smoked paprika | 2 tsp |
| Oregano, dried (Mexican if available) | 1 tsp |
| Coriander, ground | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | 1.5 tsp to start |
| Bay leaves | 2 |

Ground cumin and coriander are already reduced to account for the whole seeds.

## Added after pressure cooking

- 1.5 cups frozen corn
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 3 corn tortillas (for the thickening slurry) — or 3 Tbsp masa harina
- Juice of 2 limes
- Extra hot broth for thinning, ~1 cup, optional

## Goes in before pressure cooking

- 5 oz fresh spinach, or 10 oz frozen chopped

## Garnish bar

- Diced avocado
- Monterey Jack or cotija
- Greek yogurt (in place of sour cream)
- Baked tortilla strips
- Cilantro
- Lime wedges
- **Pickled jalapeños + hot sauce — adults only**

The soup itself contains no capsaicin. All heat lives on the garnish bar.
